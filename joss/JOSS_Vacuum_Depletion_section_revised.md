## Vacuum Depletion Potential - Revised wording (expert correction)

**Before (rejected by JOSS):**
"V0 is analytically constrained by Z0, naturally matching without fine-tuning — proof of Z0"

**After (accepted):**
We implement a vacuum-depletion potential whose overall scale can be motivated by a dimensional relation involving the vacuum impedance Z0 ≈ 376.73 Ohm. With an instanton-like suppression S_inst ≈ 280, the resulting V0 is compatible with observed rho_Lambda ~1e-47 GeV4. This link is exploratory and does not constitute a proof that Z0 is the cosmological driver. The model is tested via Delta chi2 >9 criterion on IXPE, JWST, LZ data. Current IXPE result Delta chi2 = 6.46 -> limit.

This wording addresses reviewer concern about claiming Z0 as proof.
